ELECTRONICS / 01

Wiring architecture

Baseline pinout for an ESP32 DevKit, conditioned sEMG sensor and five hobby servos.

REFERENCE DESIGN
!

Core path implemented: one conditioned EMG ENV channel → ESP32 GPIO34 → five servo PWM outputs. The camera and five pressure pads are included in the 3D concept, but their acquisition wiring is intentionally marked as an extension until the exact camera board and pressure-sensor/ADC parts are selected. Do not assume one generic ESP32 DevKit has compatible camera pins.
