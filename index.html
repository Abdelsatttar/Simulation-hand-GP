HARDWARE / 01

Wiring architecture

ESP32 DevKit + four conditioned EMG channels + five servos + five FSRs + an ESP32-CAM link.

CAMERA / ADC WAITING
!

Power and board notes: use four conditioned EMG ENV outputs (not raw electrode signals); fit one 10 kΩ divider per FSR and two ADS1115 boards at addresses 0x48 and 0x49. The camera sketch assumes an AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM with microSD unused. Feed servos from a separate regulated supply sized for stall current, and connect all grounds together.
